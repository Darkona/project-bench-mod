# Project Bench

A crafting table that remembers your recipe and stores your materials.

![A small workshop with a Project Station, an archive column next to it and a chest below](images/home-1.png)

*A Project Station, an archive column and a chest below*

Crafting the same thing many times means placing every item again and again. Project Bench keeps your recipe laid out and refills the grid from its own storage. Blueprints remember recipes, and the bench can even craft on its own with redstone.

## Included in the mod

- **[Project Bench](Project-Bench.md)**: a crafting table with 18 slots of storage that keeps its recipe laid out.
- **[Project Bench Slab](Project-Bench-Slab.md)**: half a bench that joins its neighbour into a double bench.
- **[Project Station](Project-Station.md)**: the double bench, with two grids and shared storage.
- **[Auto Project Bench](Auto-Project-Bench.md)**: crafts once on each redstone pulse, with an ejecting version.
- **[Blueprint](Blueprints.md)**: remembers a recipe and shows where each ingredient goes.
- **[Blueprint Archive](Blueprint-Archive.md)**: stores blueprints and lists them in the bench's window.
